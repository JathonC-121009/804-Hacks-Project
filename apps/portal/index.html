            What this does not do
            
      
            Written down so we say it on stage before a judge asks.
            
    
            
    
              
      
            • These are estimates, not measurements. You cannot measure the energy of a hosted model's response from outside it. Every figure is a range from a published factor, and the range is the honest part.
              • 
      
            • Average grid intensity, not marginal. Marginal is the theoretically right signal for load shifting. Average is what free hourly data supports. That gap is a known limitation and the next step.
              • 
      
            • Input energy is counted as zero. A deliberate output-centred assumption, because generation dominates inference energy. It means prompt trimming shows as a money saving, not an energy one.
              • 
      
            • Hidden reasoning tokens are invisible in web chat, so output there is undercounted.
              • 
      
            • Brevity can cost quality. That is what the gate is for, and it only covers tasks with a checkable answer.
              • 
      
            • A paraphrased repeat is not detected. Catching "summarise this" and "I'd like a summary of this" needs sentence meaning, which would mean a model call: energy spent to save energy, and your text leaving the device. Out of scope on purpose.
              • 
      
            • We never detect hallucinations. Nothing here claims to.
